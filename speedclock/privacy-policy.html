Privacy Policy

Scramble (package com.seegmillerstudio.scramble)
Effective date: September 24, 2026
Last updated: September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Scramble (“the App”), published by Seegmiller Studio (Jason Seegmiller) (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use the App on Android or iOS.

1. Summary

  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • Game settings, presets, match history, and trial status are stored on your device.
  • If you use the free tier, Google AdMob may show ads and process advertising-related identifiers and device signals under Google’s policies.
  • If you purchase Scramble Pro, payments are handled by Google Play Billing or Apple In-App Purchase, with subscription status managed via RevenueCat.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally (for example via on-device storage such as AsyncStorage), unless you clear app data or uninstall:

  • Timer presets and settings you create or change
  • Match / game history and related timing data
  • Premium trial start time and related entitlement flags used to unlock Pro features during the trial
  • Preferences such as theme or notification-related choices where applicable

This on-device data is used only to operate the App for you. It is not uploaded to our own servers; we do not operate a Scramble account backend.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Optional paid features (“Scramble Pro”) are sold through the platform store:

  • Google Play or Apple process payment, tax, and store account information under their terms.
  • We use RevenueCat to verify entitlements (for example whether Pro is active) and to restore purchases. RevenueCat may process purchase tokens, anonymous app user identifiers, and device/platform metadata as described in RevenueCat’s privacy documentation.

We do not receive your full payment card number.

4. Advertising (free tier)

On the free tier, the App may display banner ads served by Google AdMob / Google advertising services. Google and its partners may collect or process:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID on Android, subject to your device settings)
  • IP address, device type, OS version, and similar technical signals
  • Ad interaction and performance data

Ad serving and personalization are governed by Google’s policies. You can limit ad personalization in your device settings (for example Android’s ads / privacy settings). Pro / active trial users do not see these banner ads in the App’s current design.

5. Notifications

If you grant permission, the App may schedule local notifications (for example reminders related to app use). Notification permission is optional. We do not use your notification permission to build a marketing profile on our own servers.

6. Permissions

Depending on platform and build, the App may request or declare permissions needed for network access (ads, purchase verification), vibration, notifications, and related platform features. Storage-related permissions, where present, are limited by platform rules and are not used to browse your personal photo library for our own analytics.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a general-audience chess clock. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it from systems we control.

8. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: delete it by clearing the App’s storage in system settings or uninstalling the App.
  • Store purchases: managed by Google or Apple; restore purchases from within the App after reinstall.
  • Ads / RevenueCat: retention follows those providers’ policies for data they process.

9. International processing

Ad and purchase vendors may process data in the United States or other countries. Where those transfers occur, they are handled under the vendors’ contractual and legal mechanisms.

10. Your choices

  • Use Pro or complete a trial to remove banner ads (current App behavior).
  • Reset advertising identifiers / limit ad tracking in system settings.
  • Deny notification permission.
  • Uninstall the App or clear app data.

11. Changes

We may update this Policy when the App’s data practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
